# Voice and wording

Write the way a kind, clear brother would explain it at the sound desk.

- **Plain words.** "Couldn't reach OBS" rather than "WebSocket connection failed".
- **Short.** One idea per sentence. Buttons are one or two words.
- **Verbs on buttons.** Start, Stop, Save, Connect. Never "OK" when a clearer word exists.
- **Say what to do next.** "Wrong PIN. Check the number shown on the host computer."
- **Calm, never alarming.** No exclamation marks in errors, no all-caps shouting.
- **Use the meeting's own names** for parts, as they appear in the schedule.
- **Respectful.** No jokes, slang or emoji in the interface.
