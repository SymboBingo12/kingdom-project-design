Motion

Calm, quick and purposeful. The artwork moves at four moments: when a screen opens, while people wait, when a job is done, and quietly on welcome and empty screens.

The rest of the time it stays still, so nothing pulls attention from the meeting.

Four moments

Each moment has one job: show where things came from, show that something is working, say "well done", or keep an empty screen from feeling empty.

Where the art stays still

Motion should never compete with the meeting, the speaker or a number someone is reading.

On stage

Stage screens have no artwork at all. The only motion there is the warning frame, and it stops with reduced motion.

On working screens

Timer controls, counting and checklists keep their art still, so attention stays on the task.

In lists

Small discs arrive with the screen, then stay still. A row of moving pictures is noise.

With reduced motion

Arrive becomes a plain fade, rings stop, and resting art stands still. To try it in Windows: Settings › Accessibility › Visual effects › Animation effects off, then reload.
