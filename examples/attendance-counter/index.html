Rules you can see in the app

  • Big targets: every button is at least 44 px; the main one is the biggest (Fitts's Law).
  • Few choices: three tabs, one main action per screen (Hick's Law).
  • Familiar: a top bar and bottom tabs, the patterns people already know (Jakob's Law).
  • Never colour alone: messages carry an icon and words, not only amber or green.
  • Calm motion: quick, small, and switched off when "reduce motion" is on.
