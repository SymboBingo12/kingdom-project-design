The rules we keep

Art makes the tools warm and memorable. These rules keep it from getting in the way.

Art goes here
  • Welcome and first-time setup
  • Headers that fade into the page
  • Empty screens, waiting and "done"
  • Small circles that tell tools apart
Art stays out
  • Behind numbers or buttons read fast
  • On stage while someone is speaking
  • In error messages
  • More than one large piece per screen
For everyoneBig text and buttons, strong contrast, never colour alone.
Plain wordsShort sentences, and always the next step.
Our own workOriginal artwork only. No logos, names or art from the organization.
